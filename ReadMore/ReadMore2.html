Manhwa with Anime Adaptations

Explore manhwa series that have been adapted into anime, bringing their epic stories to life on screen.

Solo Leveling

Solo Leveling has been adapted into a highly successful anime series by A-1 Pictures, with multiple seasons covering the webtoon’s storyline and plans for a final season and potential movie.

Overview of the Anime

The Solo Leveling anime is based on Chu-Gong's web novel and the popular webtoon series, originally serialized in 2018. The anime adaptation was produced by A-1 Pictures and debuted in 2024, bringing the story of Sung Jinwoo, a weak E-rank hunter who gains extraordinary powers, to life. The anime has been extremely popular, becoming the most-watched anime in Crunchyroll’s history and winning multiple awards at the 2025 Crunchyroll Anime Awards, including Anime of the Year and Best Action.

Seasons and Story Coverage:

Season 1 adapts the first 45 chapters of the manhwa, covering Jinwoo’s journey from a weak hunter to gaining the powers of the “Player” system, including the D-Rank Dungeon Arc and the Reawakening Arc.

Season 2 continues the story, adapting subsequent chapters and expanding on Jinwoo’s challenges and growth as a hunter.

Season 3 is expected to cover chapters 132–179 of the manhwa, concluding the main storyline. The remaining epilogue chapters may be adapted into a final movie or a few extra episodes, completing the Shadow Monarch’s story.

Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

The anime adaptation of "Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint" has been officially announced, produced by Aniplex and Crunchyroll, with a teaser video released.

Overview of the Adaptation

The anime adaptation of "Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint," based on the popular web novel by Sing Shong, was announced during the Crunchyroll Industry Panel at Anime Expo 2024. The adaptation is highly anticipated, given the web novel's success and its transition into a webtoon format. The production is being handled by Aniplex, a well-known studio in the anime industry, although the specific animation studio involved has not yet been disclosed.

Story Synopsis

The story follows Kim Dokja, an ordinary office worker who is the sole reader of a web novel titled "Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse." When the events of the novel begin to unfold in reality, Dokja finds himself in a post-apocalyptic world where he possesses unique knowledge of the story's plot and outcomes. This knowledge becomes his greatest asset as he navigates the challenges of this new reality.

The Beginning After the End

The Beginning After the End anime premiered on April 2, 2025, produced by Studio A-CAT, and is available on Crunchyroll and Fuji TV.

Overview and Production

The Beginning After the End (TBATE) is an adaptation of the web novel and webcomic created by TurtleMe in 2018. The anime was officially announced at New York Comic Con on October 19, 2024, and is produced by Studio A-CAT. TurtleMe served as executive producer to ensure the adaptation remained faithful to the original story’s themes and characters. The anime features Japanese audio with an English dub also available.

Plot Summary

The story follows Grey, the strongest king in history, who dies unexpectedly and is reincarnated as a powerless infant named Arthur in a magical world. Surrounded by a loving family and companions, Arthur experiences joys he never knew in his previous life. However, his family faces attacks from bandits, setting him on a journey filled with adventure, magic, and personal growth. The series blends Eastern and Western fantasy elements, with a detailed magic system and rich worldbuilding.

The God of High School

God of High School is an anime adaptation of the popular South Korean webtoon, produced by MAPPA, which aired from July to September 2020.

Overview

The God of High School is based on the webtoon written and illustrated by Park Yongje. The anime adaptation was produced by the renowned Japanese animation studio MAPPA in collaboration with Crunchyroll and Webtoon. It premiered on July 6, 2020, and is available for streaming on platforms like Crunchyroll, Vudu, Prime Video, and Apple TV.

Plot Summary

The story follows Jin Mori, a high school student and martial artist, who enters a tournament called "The God of High School." This competition seeks to find the strongest fighter among Korean high school students, with the winner granted any wish they desire. Alongside his friends Han Dae-Wi and Yu Mi-Ra, Mori faces various opponents, each with unique fighting styles and motivations. As the tournament progresses, they uncover a deeper conspiracy involving a mysterious organization and the supernatural powers of gods and demons.
